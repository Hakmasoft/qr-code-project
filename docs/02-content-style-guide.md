# Content Style Guide — Soul Hub

## 1. Purpose

This guide locks the rules for every piece of written content in the project: surface lines, clip scripts, season ads, and Hub copy. It exists so that every contributor — human or agent — produces content that sounds like the same project.

The single most important principle: **the surface determines the response.** Everything downstream depends on whether one line makes someone stop.

## 2. The Three-Layer Content Model

| Layer | Job | Length | Tone |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Surface | Make someone stop and scan | 1–2 lines | Human, relatable, unresolved |
| Clip | Answer the curiosity, make the connection | 60–90 seconds | Warm, honest, meaningful |
| Season Ad | Offer a concrete next step this season | 10–20 seconds | Welcoming, specific, no pressure |

Each layer has a distinct job. Do not let one layer do another's work.

## 3. The Core Rule: Surface = Human, Clip = Spiritual

**Surfaces carry no explicitly religious vocabulary.**

Banned words on surfaces (non-exhaustive):
- Jesus, God, Christ, Lord, Savior
- Salvation, saved, sin, repent, redemption
- Church, gospel, scripture, Bible, verse
- Faith, blessing, anointed, holy
- Worship, prayer, preach

**Rationale:** Religious language triggers filtering before curiosity can work. The person categorizes the message as "religious material" and stops reading. A human line keeps the door open for two more seconds — which is all a scan needs.

**The clip does the spiritual work.** That is where scripture, meaning, and the connection to Phaneroo appear. The surface is the doorway; the clip is the room.

**The clip must honor the surface's promise.** If the surface asks "Why does success feel empty?", the clip engages that question. No bait-and-switch.

## 4. Hook Styles

Three styles, each matched to context. A surface line must be clearly one of these.

### Question
Best for: casual, high-traffic surfaces (cups, public cards)
Example: "What if the thing you're chasing is chasing you?"

### Cliffhanger
Best for: private, receptive moments (hotel rooms, late night)
Example: "This was written by a man in prison. What he says next…"

### Invitation
Best for: time-bound pushes (conference build-up, service)
Example: "There's a room full of people who get it. →"

**Mixture is allowed, but mapped to context — not variety for its own sake.**

## 5. Surface Line Rules

A surface line must be:

- **Relatable** — names a real human experience
- **Specific** — feels true, not vague
- **Unresolved** — creates a small gap the clip fills
- **Free of religious vocabulary** — see banned list
- **Short** — readable in 2 seconds

A surface line must not be:

- Clever for its own sake (clever loses to clear)
- A slogan or a Bible verse
- A call to action like "Scan me"
- Longer than two short lines

### Do / Don't Examples

| Don't (religious-first) | Do (relatable-first) |
|:---|:---|
| "Jesus is the answer." | "You got the answer. Why does it still feel wrong?" |
| "Repent and be saved." | "What if the thing you're ashamed of isn't the whole story?" |
| "God loves you." | "What if you're not as alone as it feels at 2am?" |
| "Come to church." | "There's a room full of people who get it. →" |

Notice: the right column never says "God" or "Jesus" — but every line opens a door the clip can walk through.

## 6. QR Code Placement Rules

- The QR code must never appear alone. It always sits next to the surface line.
- The value must be stated next to the code — the surface line is the value.
- Phaneroo's name or logo appears on the surface, near the code. Trust is the gate; branded codes get higher scan rates.
- The QR must never be the largest element on the surface. The text leads; the code follows.
- No third-party domains. The printed URL is always on a domain we control.

## 7. Clip Script Rules

- **Length:** 60–90 seconds maximum. Under 60 is better for cold traffic.
- **Opening:** connects directly to the surface line's promise. No generic welcome.
- **Body:** engages the human question honestly, then makes the spiritual connection.
- **Close:** transitions warmly into the season ad. Never breaks the mood.
- **One clip per topic.** Clips are evergreen; they do not change when the season changes.
- **Tone:** warm, honest, conversational. Not preachy, not salesy.

## 8. Season Ad Rules

- **Length:** 10–20 seconds.
- **Content:** a specific, current invitation — conference, service, community. Not a generic brand mention.
- **Tone:** warm, welcoming, no pressure.
- **Purpose:** answers the unspoken "what happens if I show up?" fear.
- **Rotation:** the ad changes by season. The clip does not.
- **One ad per season**, not one per clip.

### Season Ad Transition

The bridge from clip to ad must feel like the host walking you to the door, not a commercial break.

- **Warm:** "If any of this resonated, there's actually a room full of people exploring the same things this Sunday. You'd be welcome."
- **Cold:** "Phaneroo presents: The Annual Conference. Register now. Limited seats."

Same information. The first keeps the moment. The second breaks it.

## 9. Hub Copy Rules

- **Mobile-first.** Every line must work on a phone screen.
- **One primary next step, one secondary.** Never five options.
- **Load fast.** Copy must not delay the page; no heavy fonts or large hero text.
- **Instant clarity.** Within two seconds, the visitor understands what the page is and what to do.
- **No maze.** No unnecessary navigation, no pop-ups, no autoplay audio.

### Form Copy

- Four fields maximum: Name, Contact (phone or email), Topic, Consent.
- Labels plain and human. No jargon.
- Confirmation screen states a clear promise: "Someone will reach out within 24 hours." The Follow Up team must meet it.

## 10. Tone Principles (All Layers)

- **Human first.** Write to a person, not an audience.
- **Clear over clever.** Clarity wins at two seconds.
- **Warm, not preachy.** Invite, do not pressure.
- **Honest.** No bait-and-switch between layers.
- **Specific.** Vague lines convert nobody.
- **Unresolved on the surface, resolved in the clip.**

## 11. Vocabulary Reference

### Preferred words (human, warm)
life, peace, purpose, rest, anxious, empty, alone, curious, real, honest, listen, welcome, room, people, story, question, next, now

### Banned on surfaces (religious)
God, Jesus, Christ, Lord, Savior, salvation, sin, repent, church, gospel, Bible, verse, faith, holy, worship, prayer, preach

### Allowed in clips and season ads
The full range of scripture and ministry vocabulary is permitted in the clip and season ad. The restriction applies to surfaces only.

## 12. Review Checklist

Before any content goes live, confirm:

- [ ] Surface line is free of banned religious vocabulary
- [ ] Surface line is relatable, specific, and unresolved
- [ ] Surface line and clip are written as a pair
- [ ] Clip honors the surface's promise within the first 15 seconds
- [ ] Clip runs 60–90 seconds or less
- [ ] Season ad is current, specific, and 10–20 seconds
- [ ] Transition from clip to ad is warm, not commercial
- [ ] QR code is next to the text, not alone
- [ ] Phaneroo name or logo appears near the code
- [ ] No third-party domains on any printed material
- [ ] Hub loads in under 3 seconds
- [ ] One primary next step, one secondary
- [ ] Form is four fields maximum
- [ ] Confirmation screen states a response-time promise
